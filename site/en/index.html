Why I made it

After Google Assistant became Gemini, even small things like “set an alarm” or “turn on the flashlight” go through a large AI model.

I only need simple actions, not a model that powerful. So Say & Done handles common commands right on the phone: it acts as soon as you finish speaking, with no cloud round trip, no sign-in and no AI bill.

Only when it meets a sentence it does not understand can it hand that sentence to your own AI key, using the smallest, fastest model by default.

Install and set up

  1. Download and install the APKIt is not on Google Play. Open this page on your phone, tap “Download APK”, and allow your browser to install unknown apps when asked.
  2. Make it your default assistantOpen Say & Done. The first item under Settings takes you to the system’s “Default digital assistant app”; choose “Say & Done”.
  3. Allow the microphoneUsed to hear you. Contacts, location, calendar and other permissions are optional.
  4. Choose how to call it“Ways to call the assistant” on the Help tab has a button for each method, for example setting “Press and hold power button” to the digital assistant.
  5. (Optional) Add an AI keyIt works without one. To have it answer general questions, expand “AI (optional)” under Settings, add a key and tap “Test connection”.

Questions

Why is there no wake word?

The low-power chip that listens for wake words is reserved for the phone’s built-in assistant. A third-party app would have to keep the microphone and processor running all the time, which drains the battery and leaves the microphone indicator on. So Say & Done uses the power button, Quick Tap and widgets instead.

Do I need an AI key? Does it cost money?

No. Alarms, timers, volume, silent mode, flashlight, brightness, Do Not Disturb, music, opening apps, navigation, calls, weather and routines are all handled on the phone.

With a key, only sentences the rules do not understand are sent out, billed by the AI service you choose. The default is the smallest, cheapest model, so each sentence costs very little.

Which languages does it understand?

English and Traditional Chinese (Mandarin). It follows your phone’s language: on a Chinese phone the screens, voice commands and spoken replies are in Chinese; on any other language they are in English. English voice commands are new in 0.4.0, so if a sentence is not understood, try a simpler wording or tell me by email.

Does it work on the lock screen?

Holding the power button works on the lock screen too. Commands that open another app, such as navigation, ask you to unlock first. This is an Android security rule.

Is what I say uploaded?

Speech recognition is done by the Google speech service on your phone. Say & Done itself collects nothing and has no analytics.

Only three things are ever sent out: with an AI key, the one sentence the rules do not understand goes to the AI service you chose; with a YouTube key, the song or artist you asked for is looked up on YouTube; and a weather check sends your approximate location to the Open-Meteo weather service. Keys stay on your phone and are not backed up or transferred.

I installed 0.2.x. How do I update?

From 0.3.0 the package name changed along with the name Say & Done, so your phone treats it as a different app and it cannot be installed over the old one.

Install the new version, make it the default assistant, set up your routines and keys again, then remove the old app. Later updates install normally.

Which phones are supported?

Android 12 or later with the Google speech service. Developed and tested on a Pixel 7 Pro (Android 17). Quick Tap and lock screen widgets are Pixel features; other brands have their own settings.
